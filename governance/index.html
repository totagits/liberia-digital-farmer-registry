How it works

Controlled from field activity to national decision

  1. 01

    Institution submits

    The responsible contributor records content and evidence under its official institutional account.

  2. 02

    Steward reviews

    The dataset steward checks standards, quality, authority, sensitivity and required corrections.

  3. 03

    Authority approves

    The designated approving institution records a signed decision and publication conditions.

  4. 04

    System monitors

    Review dates, exchanges, incidents, decisions and downstream use remain attributable and auditable.
