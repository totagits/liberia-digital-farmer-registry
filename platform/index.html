How it works

Controlled from field activity to national decision

  1. 01

    Register once

    Capture the person or organization, household, farm, parcels, production and consent through an entity-tailored workflow.

  2. 02

    Verify and approve

    Supervisors, institutional stewards and GIS officers assess identity, completeness, uniqueness and spatial quality.

  3. 03

    Serve many times

    Authorized programmes use the approved record for extension, targeting, vouchers, inputs, referrals and payments.

  4. 04

    Monitor and improve

    Audit trails, quality indicators and outcome reporting support accountable decisions and continuous improvement.
