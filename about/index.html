About the Digital Farmer Registry

A government-led foundation for agricultural modernization.

The DFR is designed as national digital public infrastructure that improves farmer visibility, agricultural planning, service coordination, social-protection targeting and evidence-based policy.

How it works

Controlled from field activity to national decision

  1. 01

    Assess

    Inventory existing registries, programmes, systems, standards, capacities and institutional responsibilities.

  2. 02

    Design

    Agree the data model, architecture, governance framework, safeguards, SOPs and implementation plan.

  3. 03

    Implement

    Configure the platform, migrate validated data, test integrations, train users and pilot operational workflows.

  4. 04

    Scale and sustain

    Roll out nationally, measure performance, transfer knowledge and govern continuous improvement.
